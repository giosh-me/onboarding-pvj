# Welcome to PVJets

You have just joined PVJets as a Sales Development Representative. Before you write the first line of chat with a customer, you need to know who we are, where we come from, and why every operational choice we make has a reason. This module gives you the lens through which to read everything that follows.

## Who PVJets is

PVJets is a European private aviation broker with over twenty years of experience. The single sentence we use to describe ourselves is this:

<KeyFact>
  An Italian company with Swiss excellence at its core.
</KeyFact>

It's not a brochure tagline. It's an operational choice: the founders and the senior brokers come from top-tier Swiss institutions, and that rigour — on safety, on processes, on the client relationship — is how we operate every day. The Italian part is the service culture: attention, warmth, the ability to personalise. We don't compete on "we're the most technological" or "we're the cheapest". We compete on the fact that, when things go to plan, everything is impeccable, and when things get complicated, we're there.

PVJets is formally recognised by **EBAA** (European Business Aviation Association) and **NBAA** (National Business Aviation Association), the two industry reference bodies. For a customer, knowing the broker is an EBAA/NBAA member is a strong reliability signal.

## The Co-Founder's message

The company profile opens with the signature of **Marco Vitali**, Co-Founder and President of Voyage Jets. The key sentence:

> Designing remarkable flight experiences has always been my passion.

And shortly after:

> What truly matters is not just the destination but the experience along the way.

This is the **real** PVJets positioning. We don't sell a flight from A to B. We sell the experience that begins when the client decides to leave and ends when they land at destination (and beyond, because there's the concierge). For an SDR this matters because the first message you send is not "we have this Falcon at price X/hour", it's "we are building your trip".

## What we do (in one sentence)

PVJets is a **broker**: we charter aircraft from third-party operators and propose them to clients, acting as agents on their behalf. **We do not operate aircraft ourselves**, and that is a legal point you'll find repeated in the disclaimer of every official document of ours (`PVJets s.r.l. is a flight broker [...] and is neither a contracting nor an indirect carrier`).

What we do, concretely:

- **Private Jet Charter** — on-demand private flights to any destination
- **Empty Legs** — already-booked repositioning flights at reduced rates
- **Group Charter** — solutions for corporate teams, sports delegations, private groups
- **Helicopter Transfer** — city-to-city links or access to remote destinations
- **Diplomatic Services** — specialised support for government officials, embassies, diplomatic missions
- **Air Ambulance** — rapid medical transport

All the modules that follow go into detail. For now it's enough to know that the **service portfolio is broad** and ranges from a Milan-Rome flight for an executive to an intercontinental medical transfer.

## Who we fly for

PVJets clients fall into two main categories:

- **Executives** — managers, directors, decision-makers who use private aviation as a work tool. They have tight schedules, punctuality requirements, the need for privacy to work in flight.
- **HNWI / UHNWI** (High Net Worth / Ultra High Net Worth Individuals) — private clients with high spending power. They fly for leisure, family, occasions. They're after comfort, exclusivity, experience.

<Callout type="info">
  The two categories speak different languages and expect different things. An executive asks "what time do we land at Linate?". An HNWI asks "how many can be on board the Challenger?". You'll learn to read the signal from the very first message in module 7.
</Callout>

Geographically we cover three macro-areas: **Europe** (Milan, Paris, Geneva, London, Zurich, Madrid, Vienna, Mykonos, Ibiza, Athens, Innsbruck, Istanbul, Ljubljana, Mallorca, Warsaw), **Americas** (New York, Los Angeles, Miami, Rio de Janeiro, Buenos Aires) and **Global** (Dubai, Riyadh, Sydney, Johannesburg, Singapore). Module 3 goes into detail.

## The promise: "Fly Private Every Time"

The official PVJets motto, which you'll find on the website, on collateral, on email signatures, is exactly this:

<KeyFact>
  Fly Private Every Time.
</KeyFact>

The operational translation: a PVJets client doesn't decide flight by flight whether to take the jet. For them, private is the default. Our job is to make that choice sustainable — on cost, on time, on service level — whatever the route.

## Your role as SDR

As an SDR, you are the **first point of contact** between PVJets and a potential client. Your job is not to close the flight (the senior broker does that). It is:

1. **Listen** to the request that comes in (WhatsApp, web form, email)
2. **Qualify** — understand who they are, what they want, whether they're really our target
3. **Collect** the minimum information to hand the lead off to a broker (route, dates, pax, segment)
4. **Communicate** in the right tone, which is the PVJets tone — not bureaucratic, not casual, **professional and warm**

This journey gives you all the context to do those four steps well. From here on, module by module, we build the tools.
