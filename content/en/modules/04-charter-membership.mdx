PVJets sells private flight through **two commercial models**. Understanding the difference is critical for an SDR because it determines what to promise the client, how to structure the response and — importantly — when to suggest to the broker the client's progression from On-Demand to Membership.

## The On-Demand Charter model

> Private jet travel with no commitments, real-time pricing, and last-minute availability.

It's the entry model for **anyone new** to PVJets. Main characteristics:

- **Pay-per-flight** — pay one flight at a time, no subscriptions
- **No deposit** — no upfront payments, you book the single flight
- **Real-time pricing** — the quote reflects market at that moment (operator availability, aircraft type, slot)
- **Access to 3,600+ aircraft** worldwide
- **24/7 support, no queues, swift departures** — standard PVJets operational benefits
- **Strict cancellation** — flexibility is limited, cancellations carry penalties

It suits:
- Clients who fly **occasionally** (1-3 flights/year)
- Clients who want to **try** PVJets before committing
- Clients with **time flexibility** (so they can grab Empty Legs)
- Companies with **unpredictable volume**

## The PVJets Club (Membership) model

> After being our frequent flyer for over 6 months, enter our exclusive members-only program.

PVJets Club is **not buyable from day one**. It's an invitation-based programme, reserved for clients who have already flown with PVJets for **at least 6 months** as frequent flyers. So: no new prospect enters Club directly.

What Club offers:

- **Personal flight credit account** — a personal credit balance, instant booking with no payment delays
- **Guaranteed access** — guaranteed access even in peak season (the opposite of On-Demand, where you're "subject to slots")
- **Exclusive rates** — pre-negotiated preferential rates
- **Complimentary airport transfers** — transfers to/from airport included
- **VIP concierge support** — dedicated 24/7 concierge with priority handling
- **Invitations to exclusive worldwide events** — private events (motorsport, gala, fashion, sport)

The underlying commercial model is **deposit-based credit voucher**: the client deposits an amount that's drawn down per flight. It's a prepayment in all but name.

## What this means for an SDR

<Callout type="note">
  You don't sell PVJets Club at first contact. You sell On-Demand Charter. Your job with Club is to **recognise** the On-Demand clients on the path to becoming loyal — high frequency, repeated routes, positive feedback — and flag them to the broker for Club assessment.
</Callout>

Three concrete signals that an On-Demand client is a Club candidate:

1. **Volume**: 3+ flights in 3-4 consecutive months
2. **Predictability**: recurring routes (e.g. Milan-London every two weeks), stable pax
3. **Sensitivity to non-price benefits**: asks about concierge, transfer, custom catering — signal that they value experience over unit cost

When you see those signals, flag them to the broker with a note: "candidate Club — verify". You don't propose it to the client. You only open the window for the broker.

## Empty Legs as a commercial variant

Important to close: **Empty Legs** are an On-Demand sub-category. They are repositioning flights already scheduled that PVJets sells at a discount to fill the leg that would otherwise return empty. They are the way to **lower the average price** for budget-sensitive clients with date and route flexibility.

When a new prospect arrives with tight budget and flexible dates, that's the time to ask "would you be open to an Empty Leg option?". Note it in the broker handoff. Module 7 goes into the market and client segments in detail.
