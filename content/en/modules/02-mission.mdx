# Mission and core values

In module 1 you saw that PVJets defines itself as "an Italian company with Swiss excellence at its core" and that the motto is "Fly Private Every Time". In this module we get into the **how** of that positioning: the five operational values you'll find printed on commercial slides, and the three pillars (Expertise, Safety, Trust) that hold up the promise.

For an SDR this is not "brochure philosophy". These are the criteria you use to decide how to respond to a client, what to promise and what not to promise, when to escalate to the senior broker.

## The five operational core values

### 1. Swift & Exclusive Access

> We secure peak-season slots and reach destinations others can't through top-tier partnerships.

In private aviation, "access" isn't trivial. There are **scarce airport slots** during peaks (Ibiza arrivals in August, Cortina/Innsbruck at Christmas, Mykonos in July), regional airports with operational limits, airports closed to commercial traffic. PVJets sells the fact that it has the right partnerships with operators and handlers to **unlock** that access at the last minute.

Operationally for you: if a client asks for a route or date that seems "impossible", the default is not "no". The default is "let me check with the broker". Only PVJets knows what's really accessible at that moment.

### 2. Dedicated Private Aviation Advisor

> Your personal expert available from booking to landing, ensuring a flawless experience every step of the way.

Every PVJets client has **one single point of contact** through the entire journey: from the first message to landing (and beyond, on any return). Not a call centre. Not a ticket.

Operationally for you: even if you're the first contact, the final advisor will be a senior broker. But to the client, they are talking to "PVJets". The tone you set is the tone the client associates with the brand. It doesn't break.

### 3. 24/7 Global Support

> Round-the-clock assistance to meet every request, ensuring seamless travel anytime, anywhere.

Real 24/7 — not "24/7 in words but no one answers Saturdays". Clients fly weekends, nights, on different time zones. PVJets has covered on-call shifts.

Operationally for you: when a message arrives outside standard hours, the client expects a quick response. It doesn't mean it has to be you answering at 3 a.m. — it means the PVJets system is designed so someone responds. If you're on shift, respond. If not, follow the escalation procedure.

### 4. Full Access to our Services

> Private Jet Charter, Group Charter, Empty Legs flight, Helicopter Transfers, Diplomatic Services, and Air Ambulance.

These are the **six global services** you already saw in module 1. The value here is that the client doesn't need to switch provider depending on flight type: **PVJets covers the full spectrum**. Same advisor for the Milan-Rome work flight, the heli-transfer from a private heliport, and the urgent Air Ambulance.

### 5. Sustainability Commitment

> Offset CO₂ emissions and support a greener future with our eco-conscious aviation initiatives.

PVJets compensates CO₂ emissions via certified carbon offset programmes (reforestation, renewables, carbon capture) and prioritises operators with newer, more efficient fleets. It's not greenwashing: it's a corporate editorial line. Module 6 goes into detail.

## The three pillars

Above the five values, the company profile lists **three pillars** that hold up everything else. They are more "promise to the client" than "operational checklist", but the way an SDR responds to an objection puts them constantly into play.

### Expertise

> Our Private Aviation Advisors leverage deep industry knowledge and global partnerships to deliver the most efficient, tailored charter solutions.

Twenty years in the industry, global operator network. When a client asks "what do you think is the best choice?", a PVJets advisor has an answer — not a copy-pasted list of options.

### Safety

> PVJets partners with top-tier operators who uphold the highest standards in aviation safety, ensuring strict quality control and risk management.

PVJets works **only** with operators that pass standards like **IS-BAO**, **Argus**, or **Wyvern**. These are international safety and quality certifications — they're something you learn to cite when a client asks "but who's flying this?".

<Callout type="info">
  IS-BAO, Argus, and Wyvern are not invented names: they are the three reference standards in international private aviation for safety. When a sophisticated client — especially American — asks the safety question, citing them is the right answer.
</Callout>

### Trust

> With over 20 years of experience, PVJets has built a legacy of excellence, offering reliability, transparency, and an unwavering commitment to premium service.

Twenty years of history, reputation built one flight at a time. The Trust pillar materialises in three behaviours: **reliability** (we keep what we say), **transparency** (we hide neither costs nor limits), and **commitment to premium service** (even when it means accepting a thinner margin to keep the client happy).
