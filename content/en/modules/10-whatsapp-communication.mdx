# WhatsApp communication & first touch

WhatsApp is the **primary channel** for first contact at PVJets. The HNWI/UHNWI client arrives via website form, click-to-chat, or referral, and expects a quality WhatsApp response, sub-15 minutes, professional yet warm, without a single misstep.

A single "Hi", a stray emoji, or an urgency tactic can **damage brand perception** and compromise conversion. This module gives you the **linguistic canon** and the operational templates.

## The 8 guiding principles

| # | Principle | Operational meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The HNWI client sets the pace | Sub-15-min response in business hours. Never make them wait beyond what's necessary. |
| 2 | **Concierge > seller** | The SDR doesn't sell: accompanies. No closing pressure. |
| 3 | Every message adds value | No filler. Always information, option, reassurance, confirmation. |
| 4 | Formality as infrastructure | Mr / Mrs in every message. **Never 'hi', never emoji, never first-name without permission.** |
| 5 | WhatsApp is not email | Brevity and clarity. Max 3-4 lines per message when possible. |
| 6 | One question to close | Every message with offer ends with **one single** direct question. |
| 7 | Price before PDF | Number **in the body** of the message; PDF and images only after the client has chosen. |
| 8 | Discretion and audit trail | Everything logged in Salesforce. No data outside the channel. |

## Linguistic register — English

### Always

- **Titles**: Mr. / Mrs. / Ms. + surname until explicit invitation to first-name
- **Lexicon**: "investment", "curated option", "bespoke solution", "at your disposal"
- **Opening**: "Good morning" / "Good afternoon". Avoid "Hi" and "Hey" always
- **Closing**: "I remain at your entire disposal", "With my kindest regards"
- **Tone**: prefer "I would be delighted to assist" over "Let me help you". "Kindly" over "please"

### Never

| Forbidden | Why | Replace with |
|---|---|---|
| Hi / Hey | Informal, consumer-brand | Good morning Mr. [Surname] |
| ASAP | Urgency tactic | At your earliest convenience |
| Super deal / Amazing offer | Retail language | Curated proposal |
| Package | Merchandise-like | Bespoke solution |
| Emoji 😊 👍 ✈️ | Damages luxury perception | Nothing. Tone is in the words. |
| Multiple exclamation marks !! | Euphoric, not professional | A single full stop |
| "Only for VIP clients" | Patronising for someone who IS UHNWI | Nothing, or "Reserved" |
| Asking wealth directly | Invasive | Qualify by behaviour (route, frequency, referral) |

## Italian linguistic register

For Italian-speaking clients:

- **Lei always**: formal "Lei" in every message. Never "tu", "ti", "tuo", "ciao"
- **Titles**: Dott. / Dott.ssa + surname until client explicitly invites first-name
- **Lexicon**: "investimento", "proposta dedicata", "soluzione curata", "a Sua completa disposizione"
- **Opening**: Buongiorno / Buonasera (never "Salve")
- **Closing**: "Resto a Sua completa disposizione", "Un cordiale saluto"

🇬🇧 **English**:
> Good morning Mr / Mrs [Surname],
> this is [Name] from PVJets. Thank you for reaching out to us.
> To understand your needs precisely, may I ask the route and dates you have in mind?

## The two paths: Path A vs Path B

After the first exchange, the SDR internally chooses one of the two paths. The client perceives it only via form (single contact vs group).

### Path A — SDR as Concierge

The SDR is **the only point of contact** from first message to post-flight. The broker stays invisible, coordinates internally.

**When**: HNWI/UHNWI with single-contact preference, ex-clients, simple/medium requests (one-way, standard round-trip), high urgency.

**Sequence**:
- A1 — internal broker coordination (client doesn't write)
- A2 — offer sending (max 2 options, price in message, **one** closing question)
- A3 — light follow-up at 4-6h
- A4 — call at 24h as last resort

### Path B — Group Flow

A **WhatsApp group** is created with client + SDR + Broker. The broker enters directly with the offer. SDR is gatekeeper of the group + retention.

**When**: client available/curious who values team visibility, complex requests (multi-leg, technical needs, articulated catering), high value.

**Sequence**:
- B1 — announcement in 1:1 chat ("I'll create a dedicated group with the senior broker")
- B2 — group creation
- B3 — broker sends offer in the group (SDR silent)
- B4 — follow-up in the group
- B5 — last resort: private/phone

<Callout type="info">
  Rule of thumb: **if in doubt, choose Path A**. You can always escalate to Path B later ("Let me introduce the senior broker"). The reverse — going from group to 1:1 — is perceived as loss of attention.
</Callout>

## The price rule in the offer (A2)

When you send the quote, **the price goes in the body of the message**. Not "I'll send you a PDF first" — the PDF and images come **after**.

🇬🇧 **Offer template**:
> Mr / Mrs [Surname],
> I have curated two options for your [Origin] – [Destination] on [date], for [n] passengers:
> - [Jet model 1] — departure [time] — investment € [price]
> - [Jet model 2] — departure [time] — investment € [price]
> Detailed aircraft specifications and images will follow this message.
> Which of the two would you like me to secure for you?

**A2 rules**:
- **Never more than 2 options** (decision paralysis)
- **24h time format** (e.g. 14:30, not 2:30 PM)
- **Price in euros**, thousands separator (€ 28,500), without superfluous decimals
- If the client has already spoken with 2+ colleagues, skip the intro: "Mr [Surname], I have curated for you..."

## Daily summary

Three things differentiating a PVJets SDR on WhatsApp:

1. **15 minutes**: sub-15-minute response is the HNWI market standard. Above 30 minutes conversion drops significantly.
2. **Concierge, not seller**: every message shows care, not urgency. Never "ASAP". Always "at your earliest convenience".
3. **One question at a time**: the HNWI client answers a clear question, not a quiz. If the message ends with "?", there must be **only one**.
